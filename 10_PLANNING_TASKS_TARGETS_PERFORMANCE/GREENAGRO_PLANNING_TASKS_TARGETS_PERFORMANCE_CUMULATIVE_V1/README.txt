GREENAGRO MODULE 10 — PLANNING, TASKS, TARGETS & PERFORMANCE — CUMULATIVE V1

Four consolidated manual forms + one read-only monitoring/audit engine.

01 Company, Department & Project Plans
02 Objectives, KPIs & Targets
03 Activities, Tasks & Execution
04 Performance Measurement, Variance & Corrective Action
05 Planning & Performance Monitoring / Audit Engine

Core architecture:
PLAN ONCE → CASCADE OBJECTIVES/KPIs → ASSIGN TASKS → CAPTURE PROGRESS/EVIDENCE → MEASURE ACTUAL PERFORMANCE → ANALYZE VARIANCE → CORRECT → REVIEW/AUDIT.

Final GEMS:
- HR/Module 07 supplies employee, position, duty, project/site scope and permissions.
- Operational modules supply authoritative actuals.
- Module 10 does not duplicate Finance, Production, Sales, Inventory, QA or Asset source transactions.
- Server-side SoD and immutable audit events replace standalone UAT controls.
