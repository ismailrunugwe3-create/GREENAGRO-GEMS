Module 10 Architecture Verification

Plan → Objective/KPI → Task → Evidence → Performance → Variance → Corrective Action → Audit

Verification Verdict: PASS — Standalone UAT Baseline

Four consolidated manual forms plus one read-only engine cover company/department/project plans, objectives, KPIs, tasks, employee responsibility, deadlines, evidence, measurement, variance, corrective action and management monitoring.

Authority Boundaries

Module 10 owns planning structures, targets, task assignments and performance review. Operational actuals remain authoritative in Production, Inventory, Sales, Finance, HR, QA and Assets. Module 10 references or derives those facts rather than re-entering them.

Final GEMS Controls

Module 07 controls role/duty/project/site/form/action permissions. Employee assignments auto-pull from HR and Organization modules. KPI actuals can auto-derive from authoritative source modules. Server-side SoD separates plan owner/approver, task owner/reviewer and performance preparer/reviewer. Approved plans/KPIs are version-controlled; audit events are immutable; overdue tasks/actions escalate to supervisors/managers; dashboards respect organizational scope.
