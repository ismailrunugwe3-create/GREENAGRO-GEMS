GREENAGRO Stock & Inventory — Architecture Verification Report

Cumulative V1 · Forms 1–8 + Engines 9–10

1. Architecture verdict

PASS — suitable as the standalone functional baseline for later integration into the new GEMS architecture.

The package follows the principle ENTER ONCE → VALIDATE ONCE → STORE ONCE → USE EVERYWHERE → REPORT MANY TIMES. Stock balances are derived from eligible movements rather than manually entered.

3. Verified transaction architecture

PASS:

Receipt → IN; Approved Issue → OUT; Transfer entered once and generates source OUT/destination IN; Return direction derives from return type; only submitted + approved Form 6 adjustments become ledger movements. Form 7 does not alter balance directly.

Verified automatic linkage: an approved Form 7 reconciliation requiring adjustment creates a Form 6 ADJ-xxxxxx case with physical-count evidence and keeps that Form 6 case pending its own approval.

4. Batch / availability controls

PASS:

Form 8 separates physical existence from usability. QUARANTINED, QC HOLD, REJECTED, RECALLED and EXPIRED batches are controlled. Engine 10 distinguishes Physical Stock from Available Stock and generates expiry/FEFO/recall alerts.

5. Segregation and audit controls

PASS at standalone-UAT level:

Form 6 includes evidence, reason codes, reporter/verifier/approver controls and audit trail. Form 7 includes blind count, independent counters, recount, investigation, approval and audit trail. Final GEMS should replace free-text staff names with authenticated Employee/User IDs.

7. Final verified flow

Item Master → Receipt → Batch/Release → Ledger → Issue/Transfer/Return → Production/Sales reference → Count/Reconciliation → Controlled Adjustment → Ledger → Dashboard/Reorder/FEFO/Recall.

No component requires manual entry of current stock balance.

8. Recommendation before new GEMS integration

Freeze this package as the Stock & Inventory functional baseline. During integration, preserve transaction semantics and approval rules, but replace duplicated standalone lookup fields and localStorage with shared master-data selectors, centralized transaction services and authenticated audit identity.
