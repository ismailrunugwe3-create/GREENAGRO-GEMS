Five consolidated manual forms + one read-only monitoring/audit engine.

01 Asset Register & CAPEX Acquisition
02 Asset Assignment, Transfer & Disposal
03 Inspection & Preventive Maintenance
04 Breakdown, Repair & Return-to-Service
05 Depreciation & Finance Reference
06 Asset & Maintenance Monitoring / Audit Engine

Authority rule:
Procurement owns PR/PO/supplier commercial commitment.
Inventory owns physical receipt and spare-parts issue.
Finance owns CAPEX payment and accounting depreciation/disposal journals.
Module 09 owns asset identity, custody/location, condition and maintenance lifecycle.

Final GEMS will use central DB, authenticated identities, Module 07 permissions, server-side SoD, immutable lifecycle events and automatic cross-module references.
