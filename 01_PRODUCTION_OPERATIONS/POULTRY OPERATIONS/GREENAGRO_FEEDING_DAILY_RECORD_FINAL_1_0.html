FORM · DAILY USE
1Feeding responsibility & production group
2Feed plan, issue & traceability
Future GEMS linkage: flock population, age/stage, approved ration, feed item, stock batch and unit cost should be pulled automatically where available. The feeder records the actual feeding event and exceptions.
3Actual feeding & intake reconciliation
Feeding control: enter the plan and actual quantities to run automatic exception checks.
4Production performance context
Performance fields are optional and contextual. They support later linkage to Poultry/Goat Production records; they should not create duplicate production reporting when integrated into GEMS.
5Review, corrective action & control
Control principle: feed issued, feed actually consumed, refusal and wastage are recorded separately. This prevents feed issued from being treated automatically as feed consumed and supports future GEMS stock reconciliation.
