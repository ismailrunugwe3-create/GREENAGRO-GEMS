SYSTEM-WIDE REPORTING STANDARD
All six Poultry Operations forms now use dynamic calendar reporting.
- Today: current calendar date.
- This week: current Monday–Sunday calendar week.
- Month: selectable month and year, including prior recorded years.
- Quarter: selectable Q1 Jan–Mar, Q2 Apr–Jun, Q3 Jul–Sep, Q4 Oct–Dec and year.
- Semi-annual: selectable Jan–Jun or Jul–Dec and year.
- Annual: selectable current or prior recorded year.
- Calendar years update automatically from the current system year and years represented in saved records.
- Historical period filtering uses both exact start and end dates; records outside the selected period are excluded.
