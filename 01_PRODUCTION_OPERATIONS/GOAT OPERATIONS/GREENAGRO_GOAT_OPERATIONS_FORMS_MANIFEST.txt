GREENAGRO FOOD EMPORIUM
GOAT OPERATIONS FORMS — MANIFEST V1.0
Date: 03 October 2026

Architecture:
ENTER ONCE -> LINK / PROCESS -> DASHBOARD / REPORT

Completed forms:
1. Goat Daily Herd Record — daily population reconciliation, welfare and observations.
2. Goat Breeding & Reproduction Record — mating/service, pregnancy, kidding, weaning and reproductive follow-up.
3. Goat Growth & Performance Record — weight, weight gain, ADG, body condition and performance follow-up.
4. Goat Movement & Population Control — authoritative goat movement/population-changing transactions.

Shared production standard:
- Dynamic calendar with current/historical Month, Quarter, Semi-annual and Annual reporting.
- Calendar years automatically expand from current year and saved records.
- Exact start/end date filtering.
- Saved local register and record detail.
- CSV export.
- Validation and Normal/Warning/Critical controls.
- Supervisor review and audit timestamps.
- Mobile-responsive interface.

No-double-reporting rules:
- Goat Movement owns physical movement transactions; Daily Herd consumes approved movement references.
- Veterinary owns diagnosis/treatment/animal-health investigation.
- Feeding workflow owns detailed feed use/reconciliation.
- Daily Herd owns daily population/welfare observations.
- Breeding owns reproductive events/outcomes.
- Growth owns weight/performance measurements.
- Purchases, Sales, Inventory and financial transactions remain in their authoritative GEMS workflows.
