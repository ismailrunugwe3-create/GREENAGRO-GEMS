DATA OWNERSHIP / NO DOUBLE REPORTING
- Manufacturing Batch owns the manufacturing event and production mass balance.
- Raw Material Consumption owns actual material use against the batch; Inventory Issue
  remains the authoritative stock movement and Purchases remains the procurement/cost source.
- Quality Control owns QA findings and batch release/hold/rejection.
- Finished Feed Output owns packaging/output reconciliation and inventory handoff;
  Inventory owns stock-in and Sales owns customer/revenue transactions.
- No duplicate manual Purchase/OPEX/CAPEX/Sales financial entry is introduced.

-> Finished Feed Output -> Inventory Stock-in -> Sales/Feeding workflows.
